Installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.
 
Check the pods:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
 
Open the shop with port-forward (two terminals, keep both open):
  kubectl port-forward svc/backend {{ .Values.backend.port }}:{{ .Values.backend.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  kubectl port-forward svc/frontend {{ .Values.frontend.port }}:{{ .Values.frontend.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
 
Then open http://localhost:{{ .Values.frontend.port }}
